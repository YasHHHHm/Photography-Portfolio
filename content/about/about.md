I started photographing on a borrowed film camera during a monsoon in Kerala, and I have been chasing that same wet, silver light ever since.

Most of my work is slow travel: a week or two in one place, the same streets at different hours, until people stop noticing the camera. I am drawn to working rivers, early markets, and the quiet minutes before a city wakes up.

When I am not travelling I work as a staff photographer, teach the occasional workshop, and develop black-and-white film in a very small kitchen.
