I'm Krish — a visual storyteller, photographer, and aspiring filmmaker with a deep passion for nature, cinematography, and travel.

I'm drawn to the raw beauty of the mountains, the stillness of nature, and the little details most people walk straight past: cloud drifting through the forest above Dharamshala, a meadow coming into bloom after the monsoon, the last light on a ridge before it fades. On trails like Triund and the Valley of Flowers, I try to slow down long enough to notice them, and to show the world the way I see it.

I work in both stills and motion. A photograph holds a single moment; a clip holds the way that moment moved — mist rolling over a ridge, grass bending in the wind, a waterfall swollen with rain. Through nature videography, photography, and cinematic storytelling, I aim to turn these fleeting moments into visuals that evoke emotion and tell meaningful stories.

My creative journey is driven by curiosity, experimentation, and a constant desire to learn and grow. Every trip teaches me something new about light, composition, and pacing, and I carry it into the next one. Whether it's a breathtaking landscape, the movement of nature, or the feeling of standing somewhere extraordinary, I want my work to feel authentic, immersive, and timeless.

For me, it's never just about capturing a place. It's about capturing its atmosphere, its emotions, and the feeling of being there.

> Every frame has a feeling. Every place has a story.
